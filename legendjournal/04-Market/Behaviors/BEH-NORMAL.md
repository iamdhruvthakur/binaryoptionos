## NORMAL Market Behavior

**Category:** OTC Quotation Behavior

A **NORMAL** OTC market is characterised by:
- Price moving with regular structural rhythm.
- Key technical levels generally being respected.
- Reduced occurrence of unexpected massive wicks or deep liquidity sweeps against the trend.

## REVERSAL Market Behavior

**Category:** OTC Quotation Behavior

A **REVERSAL** OTC market is characterised by:
- Price actively hunting liquidity.
- Standard technical structures failing or breaking.
- Trapping patterns (e.g. false breakouts followed by rapid reversals).

## Trading in REVERSAL Behaviors

> Record observations about trading this behavior here. Often requires adjusting strategy or waiting for the algorithm to revert to NORMAL.
